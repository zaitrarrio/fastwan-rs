fastwan-rs

Text-to-video on your own desktop GPU, in Rust. One set of fused kernels runs through WebGPU (Vulkan, DX12, Metal: NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Apple) or CUDA, and the TAEHV decoder turns latents into frames you can watch while the clip finishes.

Run the engine

Download the model once (about 14 GB with the text encoder), then start the engine and allow this site to reach it:

huggingface-cli download FastVideo/FastWan2.1-T2V-1.3B-Diffusers \
    --include "transformer/*" "text_encoder/*" "tokenizer/*" --local-dir fastwan
huggingface-cli download madebyollin/taehv taew2_1.safetensors --local-dir fastwan
fastwan serve --model-dir fastwan --allow-origin https://fastwan.pages.dev

The engine listens only on 127.0.0.1 and answers this one site. Without --allow-origin it serves the studio itself at http://127.0.0.1:7860/.

Where it stands

  • The Wan DiT matches Diffusers to 4·10⁻⁷ and the UMT5 text encoder matches transformers to 2·10⁻⁶, on random-weight test models.
  • Every WebGPU kernel passes against a CPU reference in both wgpu and Chrome's WebGPU.
  • No clip from the real checkpoint and no real-GPU timing has been published yet. The bench page gives you your own GPU's numbers.
